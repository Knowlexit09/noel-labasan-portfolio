Make the offer easy to understand and encourage local orders.

Creative Process

From campaign idea to finished motion.

◌
01 · Concept Define the campaign system.

Identify product groups, pricing message, campaign goal, and visual direction.

▧
02 · Design Create the visual assets.

Build category-led layouts, product visuals, price hierarchy, and CTA graphics.

▷
03 · Motion Turn the campaign into video.

Sequence clips, narration, overlays, transitions, pacing, and the final order CTA.

✓
04 · Final Review for mobile delivery.

Check branding, readability, updated prices, audio timing, and final export.

Client work: Gatchalian Meatshop. AI-assisted visuals were used in parts of the workflow; final selection, layout, branding, pricing/text, sequencing, and editing were manually reviewed and assembled.

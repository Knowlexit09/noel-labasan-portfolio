Gatchalian Meatshop — Social Media Campaign

A coordinated graphic-design and short-form video campaign built around clear retail pricing, fresh product visuals, strong category grouping, and an order-focused call to action.

Canva Photoshop CapCut AI-assisted visuals
✓ Real client work · Portfolio use approved
Final campaign media is ready.

The locked campaign board and selected Meta-ready video are prepared. This staging page stays noindex until those binary assets are uploaded and wired into the repository.

ObjectivePromote fresh meat products and value pricing while driving local orders.
AudienceHouseholds, local shoppers, and reseller-oriented buyers.
FormatSocial campaign board + supporting posts + vertical Meta ad video.
01 · Brief

Make the offer easy to understand at a glance.

The campaign centers on pork, chicken, and seafood with clear category-led product presentation. The visual system uses Gatchalian Meatshop's red-forward identity with white, warm-gold, and fresh-green accents so the promotional work feels consistent across static and motion pieces.

02 · Creative Direction

Bold retail energy without losing readability.

Large headlines, grouped products, high-contrast price badges, fresh-market imagery, and controlled benefit/CTA areas keep the campaign promotional but still readable on a phone. The approved campaign board is treated as the locked visual reference for the supporting pieces and video adaptation.

03 · Deliverables

One campaign, several coordinated outputs.

  • Main campaign board / key visual.
  • Product spotlight supporting post.
  • Order Now / CTA supporting post.
  • Vertical short-form Meta ad video.
  • Storyboard, product grouping, pricing overlays, and final CTA treatment.
04 · Video Approach

Food hook first, readable product groups next.

The video starts with an appetizing meat close-up rather than a slow logo reveal, then groups pork, chicken, and seafood instead of forcing viewers to read one product every second. Vacuum-sealed freshness, a cooked-meal payoff, and the final order CTA complete the short-form sequence.

05 · Process

AI-assisted production, manually directed and reviewed.

1 · Campaign system

Defined the visual hierarchy, product groups, pricing treatment, palette, and supporting-post roles.

2 · Visual production

Created/refined campaign artwork and used AI-assisted visuals where useful while preserving the approved brand direction.

3 · Motion assembly

Generated selected motion shots, then assembled the final short-form ad with readable timing, voiceover, overlays, and CTA in CapCut.

4 · QA

Checked product names, confirmed campaign prices, phone readability, branding consistency, and final mobile viewing flow.

06 · Role & Tools

Concept, layout, image refinement, and video editing.

Role: campaign concept development, graphic layout, product/promo composition, image refinement, video sequencing, text/price overlays, CTA design, and final review. Core tools shown for this project are Canva, Photoshop, and CapCut. AI tools supported selected visual/motion/voice generation, with final outputs reviewed and edited before use.

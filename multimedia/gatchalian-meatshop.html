ObjectivePromote fresh meat products and value pricing while driving local orders.
AudienceHouseholds, local shoppers, and reseller-oriented buyers.
FormatCampaign board + supporting posts + 18–20 sec vertical Meta ad.
01 · Brief

Make the offer easy to understand at a glance.

The campaign centers on pork, chicken, and seafood with clear category-led product presentation. The visual system uses Gatchalian Meatshop's red-forward identity with white, warm-gold, and fresh-green accents so the promotional work feels consistent across static and motion pieces.

02 · Creative Direction

Bold retail energy without losing readability.

Large headlines, grouped products, high-contrast price badges, fresh-market imagery, and controlled benefit/CTA areas keep the campaign promotional but readable on a phone. The approved top campaign board remains the locked visual reference for every supporting piece and video adaptation.

03 · Deliverables

One campaign, several coordinated outputs.

  • Main campaign board / grouped product key visual.
  • Pork Belly / Liempo product spotlight supporting post.
  • Order Now / CTA supporting post.
  • 18–20 second vertical short-form Meta ad video.
  • Storyboard, product grouping, pricing overlays, narration, and final CTA treatment.
04 · Video Approach

Four focused beats instead of a rushed product slideshow.

The final short-form edit uses a food-first hook, a grouped pork/chicken/seafood value message, vacuum-sealed freshness/convenience, and a cooked-meal/order payoff. Product categories and prices stay readable instead of flashing one item per second, while the CTA closes the sequence clearly for mobile viewing.

05 · Process

AI-assisted production, manually directed and reviewed.

1 · Campaign system

Defined the visual hierarchy, product groups, pricing treatment, palette, and supporting-post roles around the approved key visual.

2 · Visual production

Created and refined campaign artwork, using AI-assisted visuals only where useful while preserving the original client branding and manually checking text/pricing.

3 · Motion assembly

Selected motion clips, generated the final narration separately for a consistent voice, then assembled timing, overlays, pacing, and CTA in CapCut.

4 · QA

Checked product names, confirmed campaign prices, phone readability, branding consistency, narration timing, and final mobile viewing flow.

06 · Role & Tools

Concept, layout, image refinement, and video editing.

Role: campaign concept development, graphic layout, product/promo composition, image refinement, video sequencing, text/price overlays, CTA design, and final review. Core tools shown for this project are Canva, Photoshop, and CapCut.

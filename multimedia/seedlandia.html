Personal Project · Game Visual Development

Seedlandia Farm Adventure

Visual and systems direction for a Roblox farming world built around planting, discovery, collection, player progression, pets, and future combat. The portfolio focus is the map language, HUD planning, and how the game’s features are organized for younger players without losing long-term depth.

RobloxGame UIWorld MapFarming SystemsVisual Direction
Design pillars

A world that grows with the player.

The visual plan is organized around three recurring questions: is it fun, does it have a clear purpose, and does it create room for progression without making the screen or economy harder to understand?

✿

Farm & grow

Starter plots anchor the first experience. Planting, harvesting, mutations, splicing, and upgrades give familiar actions a path toward deeper systems.

⌖

Explore & discover

Named biomes and landmarks create reasons to move beyond the farm: rare plants, materials, pets, resources, and discoveries live in different zones.

✦

Collect & progress

Characters, pets, discoveries, contribution systems, and later combat are intended to make older content stay useful instead of being discarded immediately.

World map

Zones with readable purpose.

The map concept separates progression areas visually so players can understand what each place is for before they memorize every system.

Mother Tree VillageSpawn, shops, NPCs, auction/community functions, and an easy home landmark.
Player LandsFour starter plots for farming, building, and early upgrades.
Green MeadowsEarly farming, gathering, and low-pressure exploration.
Whispering ForestRare plants, pets, materials, discoveries, and a more mysterious mood.
Crystal CavernRare resources and higher-value discoveries with a distinct crystal identity.
Mountain RegionStronger creatures, rarer mutations, and later-game exploration.
Duel ArenaDedicated combat space so farming areas are not visually confused with PvP/PvE play.
Future biome / expansionReserved space for Sandy Shores and later content without rebuilding the entire world layout.
Game UX direction

Keep powerful systems readable on a small screen.

HUD philosophy

The interface direction favors compact buttons, icon-first navigation, collapsible/expandable control groups, a clear experience bar, and dedicated inventory/equipment space.

  • Reduce button overlap with Roblox system UI.
  • Keep the new-player guide useful but visually smaller.
  • Hide or defer advanced contribution systems until they matter.
  • Use predictable locations for inventory, skills, quests, and navigation.

Progression & safety

The project is designed to be enjoyable for younger players while still supporting long-term systems. New mechanics are checked for fun, purpose, economy impact, abuse potential, and security implications before becoming permanent.

Portfolio scope: this case presents visual/system planning and a personal game-development process. It does not claim the concept images are final in-game screenshots or that every roadmap feature is already implemented.
